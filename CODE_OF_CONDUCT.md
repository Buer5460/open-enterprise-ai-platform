# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want Open Enterprise AI Platform (OEAP) to be a productive, welcoming and technically serious open-source community for business users, AI builders and software developers.

Participants are expected to communicate respectfully, critique ideas rather than people, and keep technical discussions grounded in reproducible facts whenever possible.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and professional.
- Assume good faith, while still asking for evidence and reproducibility.
- Give actionable technical feedback.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality and intellectual property.
- Do not publish API keys, credentials, customer data, private prompts, private repositories or other secrets.
- Clearly distinguish experimental behavior from production-ready behavior.
- Disclose security concerns privately according to `SECURITY.md`.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate disclosure of secrets, spam, deceptive contribution behavior, or intentional attempts to bypass project security boundaries are not acceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, code review, project chat spaces and other official OEAP community spaces.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, hide, reject or remove contributions or participation that violates this policy. Serious or repeated violations may result in temporary or permanent restrictions from project spaces.

For security-sensitive reports, follow `SECURITY.md` instead of opening a public issue.
